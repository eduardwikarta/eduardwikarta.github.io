I am a Research Engineer at the **Future Energy Acceleration and Translation (FEAT) Centre, A\*STAR**,
where I work on synthetic diagnostics for **Doppler backscattering (DBS)**. DBS is a microwave
diagnostic that measures turbulent density fluctuations and their velocity in fusion plasmas.

Much of this work is on **[Scotty](https://github.com/beam-tracing/Scotty)**,
an open-source beam-tracing code that acts as a synthetic diagnostic for DBS
([Hall-Chen, Parra & Hillesheim, *Plasma Phys. Control. Fusion* **64**, 095002 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6587/ac57a1)).
I am one of the main developers of Scotty v4, a major rewrite that supports Cartesian as well as
cylindrical geometry and has a new, modular solver and output structure.

I graduated in 2025 from **Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore**, with a BSc in Physics
and Mathematical Sciences (double major). I was the valedictorian of the School of Physical and
Mathematical Sciences Class of 2025 and received its Outstanding Undergraduate Award. My final-year
thesis, *Beam Tracing for Stellarators*, done as an A\*STAR Research Internship Award (ARIA) intern
at the Institute of High Performance Computing (IHPC), extended Scotty to non-axisymmetric magnetic
geometries such as stellarators.

Current projects
======
* **Scotty v4**: the rewrite became the main version of Scotty in November 2026 (`v4.0.0.dev0`).
* **Benchmarking Scotty against analytic solutions**: comparing beams launched in Cartesian geometry
  with the exact O-mode solution for a 2D linear density layer
  ([pull request](https://github.com/beam-tracing/Scotty/pull/173)).
* **Beam behaviour near the cyclotron resonance**: making Scotty handle beams that approach the
  electron cyclotron resonance robustly.

Research interests
======
* Doppler backscattering and microwave diagnostics of fusion plasmas
* Beam tracing and synthetic diagnostics, in tokamaks and stellarators
* Turbulence in magnetically confined plasmas
* Scientific software for plasma physics
