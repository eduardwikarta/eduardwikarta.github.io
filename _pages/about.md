I am a PhD student in plasma physics. <!-- TODO: university, group and supervisor(s) -->
My research is on **Doppler backscattering (DBS)**, a microwave diagnostic that measures
turbulent density fluctuations and their velocity in tokamak plasmas.

To interpret DBS measurements, I work on **[Scotty](https://github.com/beam-tracing/Scotty)**,
an open-source beam-tracing code that acts as a synthetic diagnostic for DBS
([Hall-Chen, Parra & Hillesheim, *Plasma Phys. Control. Fusion* **64**, 095002 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6587/ac57a1)).
I am one of the main developers of Scotty v4, a major rewrite that adds Cartesian as well as
cylindrical geometry and a new, modular solver and output structure.
